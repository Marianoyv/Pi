Policies

Privacy and responsible use

A practical summary of how this site handles inquiries and site data.

Commercial inquiries

You can contact Pi Development by email or WhatsApp. Those providers handle your messages; this static website does not store inquiries in an application database.

Inquiry information is used only to review the request, clarify fit and respond. Submitters are not added to a newsletter or marketing list.

Pi Development uses a practical internal retention target of no longer than 12 months unless an active business relationship or a legal obligation requires the information for longer. This is a retention target, not a claim that Gmail is configured for automatic deletion.

Site measurement

Google Analytics measures visits, traffic sources, page interactions, contact intent and site performance. By default, advanced consent mode sends cookieless measurement signals with analytics storage denied. If you allow analytics storage, Google Analytics may use analytics cookies and identifiers for fuller reporting. Advertising storage, Google Signals and advertising personalization remain disabled.

Form contents, messages, email addresses, phone numbers and complete outbound URLs are excluded from custom measurement. Google may process technical information such as browser and device details.

Security and abuse prevention

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Interactive tools run in your browser. Do not paste confidential credentials or sensitive personal information into third-party content previews.

Your choices

The site does not interrupt you with a consent banner. Use Analytics & privacy in the footer to allow analytics storage, keep cookieless measurement, or turn measurement off. The choice is remembered for up to 180 days. Turning measurement off stops future analytics events and clears analytics cookies and saved editorial attribution in this browser.

You may ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information associated with an inquiry, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
